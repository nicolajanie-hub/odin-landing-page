Cat-Free Home? Not for long!

The Cat Distribution System is dedicated to ensuring that every home has at least one feline companion, and that every stray cat finds at least one human to love.

"I just got home from work one day, and I crashed on the couch like I always do, taking a nap before ordering some dinner. But when I woke up, there was a cat curled up right behind my knees. I didn't have a cat. I had no idea whose cat it was. I lived alone and I never open my windows. I only open my doors when I go in or out of my house. I have no idea how it got in. I spent hours over the next few weeks trying to find its owners, but nobody else seemed to know where it came from, either. So... I guess I just have a cat now?"
